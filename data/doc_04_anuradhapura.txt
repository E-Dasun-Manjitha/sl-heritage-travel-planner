Anuradhapura Sacred City (Pooja Nagaraya & Atamasthana)
Anuradhapura is the first ancient capital of Sri Lanka and the heart of Theravada Buddhism, designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Known locally as the Sacred City (Pooja Nagaraya), it houses the Atamasthana (Eight Sacred Places of Veneration).
Location: North Central Province, Sri Lanka.
Entrance Ticket Cost: The archaeological site ticket costs $30 USD (approx 9,700 LKR) for foreign tourists. Individual donations are accepted at sacred stupas.
Key Sacred Sights & Atamasthana:
1. Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi: The oldest documented human-planted tree in the world, brought from India in the 3rd century BC.
2. Ruwanwelisaya Stupa: Massive white dome built by King Dutugemunu.
3. Jetavanaramaya Stupa: One of the tallest ancient brick structures in the ancient world.
4. Abhayagiri Monastery & Samadhi Buddha Statue: Famous for peaceful stone carving.
5. Kuttam Pokuna (Twin Ponds) & Mirisavetiya Stupa.
Dress Code: Strict temple dress code (Pooja Nagaraya rules). White or light-colored clothing covering shoulders and knees is required. Shoes and hats must be removed before stepping onto temple platforms.
Rules: Maintain silence near meditating pilgrims. Never turn your back directly to Buddha statues for photographs.
Transport: Reachable via Yal Devi Express train from Colombo or Kandy buses. Inside the Sacred City, rent bicycles (500-1,000 LKR/day) or hire a tuk-tuk for 4,000-5,000 LKR per day.
