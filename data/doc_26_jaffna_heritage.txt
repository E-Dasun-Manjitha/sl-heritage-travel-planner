Jaffna & Northern Province Cultural Heritage Guide
Jaffna, situated at the northernmost tip of Sri Lanka, offers a rich Tamil cultural experience, vibrant Hindu kovils, historic island ferries, and unique northern cuisine.
Location: Northern Province, Sri Lanka.
Key Attractions & Costs:
1. Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil: Majestic golden Dravidian Hindu temple. Free entry. High festival season in August.
2. Jaffna Dutch Fort: 17th-century star-shaped fort overlooking the lagoon. Free entry.
3. Nagadeepa Purana Viharaya & Nainativu Kovil: Sacred Buddhist and Hindu island pilgrimage reachable by regular Kurikadduwan ferry (100 LKR ferry fee).
4. Delft Island (Neduntheevu): Remote island known for wild horses, ancient baobab trees, and coral walls. Ferry: 100 LKR government boat or 1,500 LKR private boat.
5. Keerimalai Sacred Hot Springs: Natural freshwater thermal springs right next to the ocean. Free entry.
6. Point Pedro: Northernmost point of Sri Lanka with a historic lighthouse.
Dress Code: Strict Kovil etiquettes apply. At Nallur Kovil, women must cover shoulders and knees. Men must remove their shirts and enter bare-chested. Shoes must be deposited at the shoe counter outside.
Rules: No photography inside the inner temple sanctum of Nallur Kovil.
Transport: Connected via the iconic Yal Devi express train from Colombo Fort (approx 6-7 hours journey). Local tuk-tuks, rented scooters (2,000 LKR/day), or private cars are best inside Jaffna.
Must-Try Cuisine: Authentic Jaffna Crab Curry, Odiyal Kool (seafood broth), mangoes, and Palmyra sweets.
